Our Story

BEAUTY WITH A PERSONAL TOUCH

Glamora Beauty Studio was founded on a simple belief — that every woman deserves to feel confident, pampered and completely herself. From our calm, light-filled space to our carefully selected product range, everything is designed around your comfort.

Our team of experienced beauty specialists brings years of combined expertise across hair, makeup, skincare and bridal styling. We take the time to understand your features, your lifestyle and your vision before we begin.

  • Personalised consultations for every service
  • Premium, cruelty-free professional products
  • Strict hygiene and sanitisation standards
  • Relaxing, welcoming studio atmosphere
